      Three ideas to know

      Emissions index
      Instead of tonnes, we set 2025 emissions to 100. A value of 82 simply means 18% less than 2025.
      Target path
      UNEP's 2026 report says global emissions need to fall 18% by 2030 and 45% by 2035, compared with 2025.
      Compounding
      Each year's cut applies to a smaller total, so 3% a year for 5 years is a 14.1% cut, not 15%. Small differences in the yearly rate add up over time.

      AI for Good: how this supports SDG 13

      The goal

      SDG 13 asks countries to take urgent action to combat climate change. This app focuses on target 13.2 and its emissions indicator 13.2.2, and supports target 13.3 on climate education and awareness.

      Why a simulator

      Targets are usually reported as single global numbers. Turning them into tonnes for each country, ranked by where the gap is largest, helps investors, managers and communities decide where action and capital have the most environmental impact.

      What it is, and isn't

      This tool combines observed country data, a statistical trend model (log-linear regression with 95% intervals) and a transparent, rule-based recommendation engine. It is not a trained machine-learning model or an official UN projection, and it does not estimate the causal impact of specific policies.
